What is Express Bazaar?

E-Commerce Platform

A fully functional e-commerce website featuring dynamic product rendering, add-to-cart functionality, and cart management — all built without any framework.

localStorage Integration

Cart data persists using localStorage so your items are saved even after refreshing the page. No backend needed — pure frontend magic.

Auth Flow

Sign In and Sign Up pages with basic authentication logic. Protected cart — users must log in before adding items to cart.
